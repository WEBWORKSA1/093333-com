What to compare before you sign

Delivery, not price

A cheap “grey route” that drops 15% of OTPs costs more in lost sign-ups than it saves. Ask for delivery-rate reports per operator.

Sender ID rules

Operators in the Philippines and Vietnam require registered alphanumeric sender IDs for branded traffic. Confirm lead times and who files the paperwork.

Portability-aware routing

Routing by prefix alone fails on ported numbers. A lookup (HLR/MNP) step before sending improves delivery and cuts cost.

Fallbacks

For OTPs, ask about automatic fallback to voice, WhatsApp or Viber when SMS fails.

Data protection

Check compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act and Vietnam's personal-data rules, and where message logs are stored.

Anti-spoofing

Your customers are being taught to distrust links. Plan messages that never require one, and register your brand to reduce impersonation.
