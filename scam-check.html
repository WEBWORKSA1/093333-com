How the analyzer scores a message

Each rule represents a tactic that shows up again and again in scam reports. The score is additive and capped at 100: a message with a link, a deadline and a request for a code will score high even if it looks professional. A low score is not a guarantee — it means none of the common markers appeared.

SignalWeightWhy it matters
Link in the message+30The strongest single marker. Philippine authorities summarize their advice as: if there is a link, treat it as a scam.
Asks for OTP / PIN+25No real bank, wallet or telco ever needs your one-time code.
Prize / reward+15You cannot win a raffle you did not join.
Job or investment bait+15“Like videos for ₱3,000 a day” and guaranteed-return crypto are top scam categories.
Deadline or threat+12Urgency is engineered to stop you from verifying.
Payment / fee / delivery story+10 eachSmall “fees” are the hook for card theft.
Scammers now inject texts into genuine-looking brand threads using illegal cell-site simulators, so even a message that appears under your bank's name can be fake. Always open the official app directly instead of tapping a link.
