## 2. Concept chosen: **093333 — Number Intel**
"Know the number before you answer." A free, privacy-first mobile-number intelligence + anti-scam toolkit for the 09-number world (PH, VN, TW).

**Why this beats alternatives**
- *Angel-number / numerology site* → thin content, low RPM, AdSense "low value" risk. Rejected.
- *Generic phone directory / reverse lookup* → privacy/legal risk, AdSense policy risk. Rejected.
- *Number Intel* → genuine utility (tools), evergreen + news-driven traffic, strong E-E-A-T safety content, AND a high-ticket B2B lead-gen engine (telecom/CPaaS referrals pay $25–$300+ per qualified lead).

**Revenue stack**
1. AdSense auto ads (telecom, fintech, security, insurance advertisers).
2. B2B lead gen (business.html multi-step quote form) → provider referral fees/rev-share.
3. Sponsorships (tool sponsor, contest partner, provider listings).
4. Donations/pledges (ops, promotion, hiring, prizes).
5. Exit value: domain/site sale via web.works/contact banner on every page.

**Features adopted**
- Single-field hero lookup with instant result (Getcontact, NumLookup).
- Live social-proof / counters & ticker (Getcontact "people looking up now").
- Report-a-number form + "latest reports" community loop (Phonely, 800notes, ScamAdviser).
- Scam IQ quiz → gamified retention (ScamAdviser Scam IQ Test) → turned into monthly prize contest.
- Screenshot/text analyzer (ScamAdviser Lens) → client-side scam-text analyzer.
- Business/API upsell (ScamAdviser Biz, Numverify, Twilio) → lead-gen quote form.
- Searchable prefix tables + FAQ (FilipiKnow, Vietnamese telco blogs).
- Country dialing tables (MyCountryMobile, sent.dm).
- Recovery checklist & official hotlines (FTC, CICC, eSafety).
- Footer legal stack, takedown notice, advertise page (ScamAdviser).

**Gaps we exploit**: competitors are single-country, ad-cluttered, or push apps. None combine PH + VN + TW, client-side privacy, scam-text analysis, vanity scoring and B2B quotes on one fast static site.
